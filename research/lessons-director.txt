Never leave a frame near-empty while the narrator is speaking: every key phrase (stat, rating, quote intro) needs its visual on screen from the first frame and held until the line ends. Check keyframes at each narration cue for blank pages.
Keep the picture in step with the voice: headings, labels and on-screen questions should match what is being said at that moment and help the viewer follow where the story is going, not introduce a question or point the narration never makes.
Time each reveal visual to its narration cue and in the storyboard order. Show the big number, the object or the quote card on the cue word, hold it until the line ends, and don't show later text early.
Before final cut, check that no two on-screen labels overlap and no stamp or sticker covers a label that names a key subject. Move or shrink the overlay until every label reads clearly on the frame where it appears.
Before final cut, check every on-screen text element for legibility on a phone at 1080 wide: source chips and footnotes at least 34 px, small labels (countdown, counters) at least 28 px, and wordmarks, banners and edge props fully inside the frame, never cut off ('The Daily Habi'). Cut any text that cannot be made that size.
Match the host character's expression to each line's emotion and keep one art style for the character in every scene, including the outro. Use confident for a big claim, a shrug for 'no word yet', neutral or pleased for good news, and hopeful for 'fingers crossed'. Avoid mid-blink frames, flat or alarmed faces on neutral lines, and a different art style.
Keep the opening and wide shots clean: no more than a card plus one strip and caption in the first seconds, no leftover logo cards, no wordmark in the bottom 420 px or cut off, and captions on one line in one box with no strike or underline rule, capped at x 860. Every spoken line needs a caption (no gaps in the PIP shots). Use the same host art in every scene, with no black filler frame at the end.
Never put a raw web capture or screenshot on screen (error pages like 'Forbidden', pages that are mostly white space, share icons, cropped images). Use a cropped headline card in house style, or one zoomed region with text at least 40 px. If no real visual exists, draw a stat card instead.
