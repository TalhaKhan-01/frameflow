# Wide Lanes India First

## Context
Choosing [[Frameflow]]'s niche. Six candidate lanes were proposed (see [[Niche Study]]).

## Decision (Talha, 2026-10-23)
- Keep the scope **wide**: all six lanes now, more added later. Don't narrow the niche or the audience.
  1. AI news, "what it means for you"
  2. AI × money (chips, AI companies, markets, jobs and costs)
  3. Dev tool of the day (trending repos, open-source AI)
  4. How tech works (low-poly 3D explainers)
  5. India's tech scene
  6. Mix: daily news plus weekly explainers
- **Language:** English.
- **Audience:** global in the long run; **India-specific to start**.
- "Thirdspace" (the Instagram handle `@madebythirdspace`) is an experimental name for future freelancing, not the channel brand yet.

## Consequences
- The niche study compares lanes to set the **mix and priorities** within a wide channel, not to pick a single lane.
- A wide channel teaches the algorithm its audience more slowly ([[Algorithm Signals]]). The fix: consistent format and look across lanes ([[Scene Library]]), plus series names so each lane is recognisable.
- Ideas sources must cover all six lanes, with Indian sources added.
