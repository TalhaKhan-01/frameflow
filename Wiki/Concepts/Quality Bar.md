# Quality Bar

**In one line:** the scored checklist every video must pass before it's posted; turns [[High Quality Short]] from a feeling into checks.

## Explanation
Two layers:
- **Automatic checks (code):** length, 1080×1920, loudness about −14 LUFS, no black frames, captions match the audio (Whisper).
- **Review (one model call):** hook strength, angle, pacing, payoff and accuracy, scored on key frames plus the script.

Updated weekly from the feedback loop ([[Algorithm Signals]]) and first calibrated by the niche study.
