# Algorithm Signals

**In one line:** YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels test each video on a small audience and widen reach when these signals are strong.

## Explanation
Neither platform publishes its formula; this is what each has said publicly, plus what creators consistently observe.

| Signal | YouTube Shorts | Instagram Reels |
|---|---|---|
| Stop the scroll | Viewed vs swiped away (the biggest early signal) | Watching past the first seconds |
| Watch time | Average percentage viewed; above 100% = rewatches | Watch time (Instagram says this matters most) |
| Sharing | Shares, comments, likes, subscribes | Sends per reach (DMs), key for reaching non-followers |
| Satisfaction | Fewer "don't recommend" | Saves, likes per reach |
| Originality | Re-uploads and mass-produced content limited | Reposts and other platforms' watermarks pushed down |
| Audience fit | A consistent topic teaches who to show it to | Same |
