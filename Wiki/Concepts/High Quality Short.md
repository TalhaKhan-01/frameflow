# High Quality Short

**In one line:** a Short people watch to the end and feel was worth it; polish only matters when it serves that.

## Explanation
Ranked by impact:
1. **Idea and angle:** timely, one sharp point (an angle, not a news summary), for a specific viewer.
2. **Hook (first 1–2 s):** stops the scroll with a claim, number, contradiction or question. No intro or logo. Picture and words land together.
3. **Pacing:** something changes every 1–3 s; every line earns its place; it builds to a payoff that is delivered.
4. **Visual craft:** one consistent look, motion with a purpose, 1080×1920 with platform safe zones respected.
5. **Audio:** natural voice at the right pace, music under the voice, about −14 LUFS, small sound effects on reveals.
6. **Captions:** word-timed, big, key words highlighted (most people watch muted).
7. **Trust:** correct facts, sources shown, no clickbait the video doesn't pay off.
8. **Ending:** clean, ideally loops back into the start; a short, earned call to action.

Formula: right idea × strong hook × no wasted seconds × a consistent look and sound × trust.
